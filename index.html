About Maths Quest

Personal practice, made playful

Maths Quest creates short, personalised adventures based on the Irish primary maths curriculum for 1st–6th Class.

  • Choose topics keeps practice focused on material covered in class.
  • The Work it out scratchpad provides drawing space and progressive help whenever it is needed.
  • The practice mix revisits topics using first-try accuracy—never rankings against other children.
  • Beat Quest builds drums, bass, chords and melody through accurate times-table streaks.

No account is needed to play. Guest progress stays on this device; an optional parent account saves profiles, photos, preferences and completed results privately across devices. Cookie-free analytics count anonymous game starts and completions; names, photos, answers, scores and progress are never sent to analytics. Maths Quest supports classroom learning; it does not replace a teacher’s judgement.
